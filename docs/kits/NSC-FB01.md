# NSC-FB01 — Field Base

**Status:** Planned architecture

FB01 is the larger field-base system rather than a rapid grab kit.

## Current concept

- Approximately 33-inch rolling case
- Approximately 21-inch display
- 100 Ah LiFePO4 power system
- DJI Air 3S support
- Expanded radio, networking, mapping and situational-awareness capability

Detailed electrical, thermal, antenna, networking and transport designs require separate validation before procurement or fabrication.
