# NSC-GK01 — Grab Communications Kit

**Status:** Planned; detailed packaging pending

GK01 is a larger grab-and-go communications system than EK01. It may support multi-radio and multi-receiver operation that is intentionally excluded from the compact Emergency Kit.

## Current allocation

- One of the two existing DM-32UV radios
- Future multi-SDR or expanded RF-awareness capability as power, interference and packaging are validated

Final case, power, antenna, networking and device selections remain open. EK01 must not be renamed or repurposed as GK01.
